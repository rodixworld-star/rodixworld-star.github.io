ARCHIVE GUIDE

Suggestions

Ideas to help readers discover stories and writers build stronger ones.

For Readers

  • Explore different categories instead of staying with one genre.
  • Save stories you want to return to with the bookmark feature.
  • Use search and sorting to find stories that match your mood.
  • Leave room for new writers and discover original voices.

For Writers

  • Give your story a clear title and an engaging opening.
  • Choose the category that best describes your story.
  • Use readable paragraphs and dialogue to keep the story flowing.
  • Add a fitting cover image when you publish your story.
